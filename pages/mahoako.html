    • Experience And Or Skill

    Thus far Gushing Over Magical Girls (henceforth will be referred to as Mahoako) doesn't have characters who know every martial art, or really any traditional fighting things. Her experience is the total amount of fights that have happened so far, which is like, probably around 20. As will be elucidated further, it's kinda just a bunch of bullshit, but there is some stuff. Baiser excels at keep away tactics, sneak attacks, and kidnapping.

    Perhaps her best showings as a tactician is her social engineering, as it is her role both meta-narratively and regular narratively, she acts as a wall that the heroes must overcome. The dragon they must slay to become fuller people, thusly she plans scenarios and events in order to unleash the hidden potential in her idols so they can shine brighter.

    Along with this, she is able to discern tricks from other characters (namely Venalita). She's also beaten the more experienced Lord Squad who've hunted down numerous other magical girls, so that's something.

    Abilities

    Mana/Magic

    The main power source in Mahoako is Mana/Magic (used interchangeably), which is powered by emotions. Magia Baiser is like the Hulk but instead of anger it's lust. And with such great amount of power she can use her Mana to do all sorts of things.

    An interesting thing about the magic in Mahoako is that it appears to have a DBZ kinda deal where the level of strength of the character will dictate how they are effected by hax abilities and resistances. Stronger characters being able to just power through both.

    For instance, the conflict with Lord Enorme, in her regular Magia Baiser form, Baiser was unable to take control of Lord's monsters but when she got a power boost that put her above Lord, she was able to overcome their resistance and control them. Similarly, Azul in her La Verità form was able to negate their regeneration with her attakcs.

    This is not an isolated instance, more examples include:

    • Nemo's shadows being able to stop Baiser from moving but completely control the weaker Kiwi.

    • Sister Gigant was able to burst out of Alice's doll house with little resistance. Said houses being able to mind control and physically alter the people inside.

    • Lord's Monsters do this as well.

    • Utena in her Ugly Flower form was able to corrupt Magia Magenta who has shown resistance to mind control and transmogrification.

    • Venalita states that strong emotions could dispel Baiser's magic.

    Reuse(?)

    So a question that may have arisen from you, the reader, is whether Magia Baiser can use her transformed stuff situationally as a one time thing depending on the environment, if she brings these items with her before battle, or even if she's like Kirby where she can pop out these things whenever.

    There isn't a direct clear answer on this but as I have extensively read the manga, it seems to me, that is a mix of preparing before hand and that she can just whip em' out like Kirby.

    Take for instance her summoning Lord's Monsters, unlike when she uses her Octupus in the fight with Sulfur, there it's not clear if a portal is drawn to indicate that she prepared it before hand and is just pulling it in. As you can see, the black circle drawn doesn't have the same effect the portals are consistently drawn with, being a swirly around the edges.

    But let's just say for the sake of argument that the Mangaka just forgot that detail and it is intended to be a portal (S2 please help me out), there still are various moments where Baiser just has the item she's used before apropo of nothing.

    And personally, the most overt example to me is this one, she just pops out the scissors there.

    So either way, even if the instance with Lord's monster is a portal summoning situation, it's clear she can manifest other stuff. So I'd say overall Baiser can likely reuse the majority of the things she's turned, either through simply conjuring them herself or via portals to bring them to her.

    The only holdouts I'd say that she can't pop out are the Azul Figure, Sankakumokuba & Gag, and the Dungeon. The former two we see them poof to normal not in her possession (Utena also mentions she has to buy another one figure), and the ladder is a stationery location. Although, she could portal someone in there if she wanted.
