Experience And Or Skill

Thus far Gushing Over Magical Girls (henceforth will be referred to as Mahoako) doesn't have characters who know every martial art, or really any traditional fighting things. Her experience is the total amount of fights that have happened so far, which is like, probably around 30. As will be elucidated further, it's kinda just a bunch of bullshit, but there is some stuff. Baiser excels at keep away tactics, sneak attacks, and kidnapping.

Perhaps her best showings as a tactician is her social engineering, as it is her role both meta-narratively and regular narratively, she acts as a wall that the heroes must overcome. The dragon they must slay to become fuller people, thusly she plans scenarios and events in order to unleash the hidden potential in her idols so they can shine brighter.

Along with this, she is able to discern tricks from other characters (namely Venalita). She's also beaten the more experienced Lord Squad who've hunted down numerous other magical girls, so that's something.

Abilities

Mana/Magic

The main power source in Mahoako is Mana/Magic (used interchangeably), which is powered by emotions. Magia Baiser is like the Hulk but instead of anger it's lust, the amount of Mana/Magic she has is indicated by the Stars that appear on her body, in Magia Baiser form she usually has 2 but gains more if she feels strong emotions (usually lust).

An interesting thing about the magic in Mahoako is that it appears to have a DBZ kinda deal where the level of strength of the character will dictate how they interact with hax abilities and resistances. Stronger characters being able to just power through both.

For instance, the conflict with Lord Enorme, in her regular Magia Baiser form (2 Stars), Baiser was unable to take control of Lord's monsters but when she got a power boost that put her above Lord (Lord having 4), she was able to overcome their resistance and control them. Similarly, Azul in her La Veritas form was able to negate their regeneration with her attacks (Baiser herself should get regen negate in her own La Veritas form).

This is not an isolated instance, more examples include:

• Nemo's shadows being able to stop Baiser (2 Stars) from moving but completely control the weaker Kiwi (0 Stars).

• Sister Gigant was able to burst out of Alice's doll house with little resistance (same amount of Stars). Said houses being able to mind control and physically alter the people inside.

• Lord's Monsters do this as well (4 Star).

• Utena in her Ugly Flower (a lot of Stars lol) form was able to corrupt Magia Magenta who has shown resistance to mind control and transmogrification.

• Venalita states that strong emotions could dispel Baiser's magic.

Reuse(?)

So a question that may have arisen from you, the reader, is whether Magia Baiser can use her transformed stuff situationally as a one time thing depending on the environment, if she brings these items with her before battle, or even if she's like Kirby where she can pop out these things whenever. There isn't a direct clear answer on this but as I have extensively read the manga, it seems to me, that is a mix of preparing beforehand and that she can just whip em' out like Kirby.

Take for instance her summoning Lord's Monsters. As you can see, the black circle drawn doesn't have the same effect the portals are consistently drawn with, being a swirly around the edges, like when she uses the iconic octopus. This depiction has happened twice so likely intentional (even does the staff click).

For the sake of argument, perhaps the Mangaka just forgot that detail and it's intended to be a portal, there still are various moments where Baiser just has the item she's used before apropos of nothing.

And personally, the most overt example to me is this one, she just pops out the scissors there.

Under the impression she can just whip out Lord's Monsters, that means she could likely shoot out other combo items like Kiwi's grenades or Alice's houses. Either way, even if the instance with Lord's monster is a portal summoning situation, it's clear she can manifest other stuff. So I'd say overall Baiser can likely reuse the majority of the things she's turned, either through simply conjuring them herself or via portals to bring them to her.

The only holdouts I'd say that she can't pop out are the Azul Figure, Sankakumokuba & Gag, and the Dungeon. The former two we see them poof to normal not in her possession (Utena also mentions she has to buy another one figure), and the latter is a stationery location. Although, she could portal someone in there if she wanted.

The katakana, ブリッツエンゼル, used makes it clear what the kanji means (second part is just angel), as the first part "ブリッツ" means "blitz," now "blitz" is a lone word from the German language. One of the meanings of said word is *drum roll* lightning. Meaning that the intent for the kanji in this case is to mean lightning as that is the shared meaning of both "Blitz" and "電撃." This could also mean that the other use of this kanji also means lightning as it would be consistent with this usage.

The official translation goes with just "Blitz Angel."

Resistances

Because of how the Mana/Magic System works in Mahoako, I think it's fair to give her stuff that weaker characters have also resisted.

• Mind Control: resisted the perfume from her monster, despite the perfume creature being her minion, she does not control the effects of the perfume onced sprayed, in fact she couldn't stop the people who were controlled as a plot point so this should count toward resistance. There are other arguments for this like scaling to Magia Magenta who overcame mind control from Alice.

• Body Puppetry: resisted it as a 2 Star but should be completely immune in higher forms.

• Extreme Cold: including her webs.

• Extreme Heat: been in a lot of explosions.

• Extreme Pain: she get's her shit rocked a lot.

• Electricity (Presumably Extreme): Kiwi in weaker forms took it like a champ.

• Age Manipulation: Magia Magenta resisted being turned into a baby and Neo Alice has aged herself up so should account for both sides.

• Transmogrification/Biological Manipulation/Transmutation: Lord's Monsters at 4 Stars couldn't be turned into minions by Baiser when she was a 2 Star.

• Size Manipulation: Similarly, Lord's Monsters were not shrunk when in Alice's Doll House, she had 3 Stars at the time.

• Extrasensory Perception: using certain tools she can make her minions or herself undetectable from magical detection.

• Sleep Manipulation: was able to stay awake after ingesting a sleep potion from Imitatio.

• Empathic Manipulation: was midly effected by various potion's from Imitatio, said potions were debilitating in the past for her.
