    • Experience And Or Skill

    Thus far Gushing Over Magical Girls (henceforth will be referred to as Mahoako) doesn't have characters who know every martial art, or really any traditional fighting things. Her experience is the total amount of fights that have happened so far, which is like, probably around 20. As will be illucidated further, it's kinda just a bunch of bullshit, but there is some stuff. Baiser excels at keep away tactics, sneak attacks, and kidnapping.

    Perhaps her best showings as a tactician is her social engineering, as it is her role both meta-narratively and regular narratively, she acts as a wall that the heros must overcome. The dragon they must slay to become fuller people, thusly she plans scenerios and events in order to unleash the hidden potential in her idols so they can shine brighter.

    Along with this, she is able to discern tricks from other characters (namely Venalita). She's also beaten the more experienced Lord Squad who've hunted down numerous other magical girls, so that's something.
