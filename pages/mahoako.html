Prelude

If you want a character summary go read the Wiki, this is not that. Instead I will wax poetic about weird anime porn, as I am wont to do.

So I think an interesting element of discussion of Gushing Over Magical Girls (henceforth referred to as Mahoako), is its examination/iteration on the magical girl genre/archetype. You might expect me to say it's a deconstruction, but that's a term for food and this is a manga (slash anime). For some history, the Mahou Shoujo as a genre is quite peculiar in terms of its intersection with Otaku interest/fetishization and the Bishoujo boom of the 1980's. Although not the oldest, titles such as Minky Momo and Creamy Mami were some of the earliest to pick up an adult fanbase of otaku men, which was a shock considering the demographic they were made for. At first, shows like this had the magical transformations as a metaphor for the many things a young girl could grow into or the sorta power fantasy of being able to be an adult as a child or just like, isn't it cool to do magic lol. Ebbs and Flows. This kind of Mahou Shoujo work is referred to as "Witch" type magical girls cause of Sally the Witch and other early pioneers who had a witch theme.

As the genre expanded, one of the earliest (a bit debated) titles to be made not for little girls was one Cutie Honey. A Go Nagai work and if you know anything about the man you know that is has some sexual themes so to say. This title would influence magical girl behemoth Bishojo Senshi Sailor Moon, hey we heard that word before, which again, has some sexual themes. Loooot of panty shots in that one (especially the anime). Oh and these kinda shows are referred to as "Warrior" type magical girls cause they fight. All this to say that the theme of sexuality and magical girls is deep rooted in the history of the genre.

A response I've seen to Mahoako considering its interests, is that it is unbecoming of a magical girl property, that it doesn't represent the genre well cause of the sexual themes. Which I think is malarkey as it's a very old part of it. The series, I think, plays with that history, considering Utena is a magical girl otaku and all. Just like the otaku of old, they are her interest and she very much fetishizes them. It's a bit meta in that sense, Utena's infatuation with magical girls is that they persevere through adversity. She loves seeing them persevere so much that she becomes the adversity to see them shine ever brighter, as is the role of a villain, which she takes up. What a magical girl should be, is a big part of the series, as Utena is almost always trying to see that archetype come alive.

They even have some of that witch DNA too, as Utena gets stronger, she very apparently gets older looking. Just like Minky Momo and Creamy Mami, but with more BDSM. There is a point made about Utena, when she's Utena, and when she's Baiser acting differently. At the start of the series these two halves were more clearly separated but as the series goes on the gap closes between the selves. The dichotomy of the self and the roles a magical girl takes when they are a magical girl and a regular girl, very witch coded (some of the big ones anyway).

I get people not vibing with the series, cause it has a lot of weird sex stuff in it. But one thing I think you can't say is that it doesn't explore the genre.

If you'd like to read more on the subject, I recommend The Moe Manifesto by Patrick W. Galbraith and Beautiful Fighting Girl by Tamaki Saito.

Mana/Magic

The main power source in Mahoako is Mana/Magic (used interchangeably), which is powered by emotions. Magia Baiser is like the Hulk but instead of anger it's lust. The amount of Mana/Magic she has is indicated by the Stars that appear on her body, in Magia Baiser form she usually has 2 but gains more if she feels strong emotions (usually lust).

An interesting thing about the magic in Mahoako is that it appears to have a DBZ kinda deal where the level of strength of the character will dictate how they interact with hax abilities and resistances. Stronger characters are able to just power through both.

For instance, the conflict with Lord Enorme, in her regular Magia Baiser form (2 Stars), Baiser was unable to take control of Lord's monsters but when she got a power boost that put her above Lord (Lord having 4), she was able to overcome their resistance and control them. Reuse(?)

So a question that may have arisen from you, the reader, is whether Magia Baiser can use her transformed stuff situationally as a one time thing depending on the environment, if she brings these items with her before battle, or even if she's like Kirby where she can pop out these things whenever. There isn't a clear answer on this but as I have extensively read the manga, it seems to me that is a mix of preparing beforehand and that she can just whip em' out like Kirby.

Take, for instance, her summoning Lord's Monsters. As you can see, the black circle that is drawn doesn't have the same effect the portals are consistently drawn with, being a swirly around the edges, like when she uses the iconic octopus. This depiction has happened twice so it is likely intentional (even does the staff click).

For the sake of argument, perhaps the mangaka just forgot that detail and it's intended to be a portal, there still are various moments where Baiser just has item she's used before apropos of nothing.

And personally, the most overt example to me is this one, she just pops out the scissors there.

Assuming she can just whip out Lord's Monsters, that means she could likely shoot out other combo items like Kiwi's grenades or Alice's houses. Either way, even if the instance with Lord's Monster is a portal summoning situation, it's clear she can manifest other stuff. So I'd say overall Baiser can likely reuse the majority of the things she's turned, either through simply conjuring them herself or via portals to bring them to her.

The only holdouts I'd say that she can't pop out are the Azul Figure, Sankakumokuba & Gag, and the Dungeon. We see the former two poof to normal not in her possession (Utena also mentions she has to buy another figure), and the latter is a stationary location. Although, she could portal someone in there if she wanted.
